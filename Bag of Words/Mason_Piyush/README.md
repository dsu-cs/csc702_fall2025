Other ideas on potential implementations are to take sentence embeddings and somehow link them together to find meaning in an individual paragraph from this.

So for instance, if we have a paragraph or a group of paragraphs discussing in depth a dog eating dogfood, it may go into various maticulous details, however we could try to link this to a similar sentence that may be something like: "the dog eats its food".
