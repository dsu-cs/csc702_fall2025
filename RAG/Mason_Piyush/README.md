This led me to do some research. Apparently, although Cross-Encoders are different than strict LLMs, they are actually based on LMs. They are basically adjusted or specific language model architectures. So they are fundamentally different, but based on the same thing. The way I now think of it is that LLMs are like general purpose LMs, whereas cross-encoders are like specific-purpose LMs for reranking specifically. So they were like designed for this specific task which is why they can outperform BERT-base for reranking (https://arxiv.org/html/2403.10407v1). 

This project helped us understand the fundamental differences between these two types of architectures, their similarities, and differences. Further, the Baseline strongly outperformed the BERT-Base and surprised our hypothesis.
