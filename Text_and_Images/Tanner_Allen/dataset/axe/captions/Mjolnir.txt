<mjolnir>, Mjolnir, MyoLnir

LEGENDARY - HAMMER

Only the ones worthy might wield the
legendary hammer.

MECHANICS

Martial hammer +3, single~handed. On hit:
1d8 bludgeoning + 1d8 lightning + 1d8
thunder damage. You can also throw this
hammer (30/90). After being thrown it
returns to you. 3/day you can cast "Call
lightning"; the spells save DC is 16. While
holding Mjolnir you gain a flying speed of
60 ft.

(Requires good alignment, attunement.)
