<bladestaff>, BladeStaff, BLADESTAFF

Uncommon Quarterstaff (Versatile)

Damage: 1d6 (1d8) +1 Slashing

Bladestorm: As an action you can cast the spell Cloud of Daggers
(3rd level) with this staff. The spell lasts for one minute and requires
concentration. You can use this feature once per day.

You fill a 5-foot cube within a 60 ft range with daggers. A creature takes
6d4 Slashing damage when it enters the area for the first time on a turn
or starts its turn there.
