<eyetorch>, EyeTorch, EVERTORCH

Uncommon Magic Item

Lightbringer: This torch can burn infinitely. As a bonus action you can
speak the command word of this torch. Doing so will make an
unnaturally red flame appear. Providing bright light in a 30-foot radius
and dim light for an additional 40 feet. If you make a melee Attack with
the flame active, it deals 1 fire damage.
