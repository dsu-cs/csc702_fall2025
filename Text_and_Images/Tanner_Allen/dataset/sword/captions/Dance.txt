<dance>, Dance, Damage: 1d4+1 Piercing
Range: 20/60 ft

Choreography of Returning: As a bonus action you can call back
Dance to your hand, it flies in large loops, twirls and spins through the
air. It dodges and weaves past any objects in it’s path. You can use this
feature up to a range of 200 ft

Song and Dance: When you make successful attacks with both Song
(the rapier of this set) and Dance in one round, you deal an extra 1
Piercing damage with each weapon.

Two of a Kind: Attunement to both Song and Dance counts as a single
attunement as they are a set.
