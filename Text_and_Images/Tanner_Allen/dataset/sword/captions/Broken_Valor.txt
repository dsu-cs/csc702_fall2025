<broken_valor>, Broken Valor, BROKEN VALOR

VERY RARE - SWORD

This blade is a very old, broken bastard
sword. On one side an inscription in a lost

language the other side, a single elvish
word "Shadow"

MECHANICS

This weapon acts as a dagger until its
command word is said then, Broken Valor
casts Shadow Blade. When the spell is
cast a black shadowy blade emmits from
the end of the broken sword and now acts
as short sword with the fineness light and
thrown properties (range 20/60).Broken
Valor does base damage of a short sword
plus the spell damage. It has 5 charges
and at dawn all charges are replenished.
