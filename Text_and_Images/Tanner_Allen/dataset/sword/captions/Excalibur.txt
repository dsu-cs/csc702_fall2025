<excalibur>, Excalibur, EXCALIBUR

LEGENDARY - SWORD

The most legendary, most mysterious sword
in history - with a little side-effect...

MECHANICS

Sentient Longsword +4, lawful good.

INT +4

WIS +4

CHA +4

Damage rolls made with Excalibur make
additional 2d8 radiant damage. If you roll a
natural 20 with Excalibur, the damage dies
are not doubled, but multiplied by four.
Excalibur has four spell slots and casts
"Shield of Faith" and "Bless" on you when
you enter a battle. As a reaction, it casts
"Shield" on you. Once it casted it, Excalibur
regains all his spells slots, everytime you
roll for initiative.

However; you have to obey all rules of
Excalibur. If you don't, it unattunes itself
instantly and teleports away.

(Requires attunement, mastering all four tests
determined by the DM.)
