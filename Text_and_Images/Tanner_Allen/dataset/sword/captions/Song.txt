<song>, Song, Rare Rapier (Martial, Sword), requires attunement

Damage: 1d8+1 Piercing

Charming: You can use this rapier to cast Charm Person. The target
has to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 17). The creature makes the
saving throw with advantage if you are currently fighting it, or it becomes
charmed by you until the spell ends or someone in your party harms it.

It will know it has been charmed when the spell ends. You can use this
feature once every 3 days.

Song and Dance: When you make successful attacks with both Song
and Dance (the dagger of this set) in one round, you deal an extra 1
Piercing damage with each weapon.

Two of a Kind Attunement to both Song and Dance counts as a single
attunement as they are a set.
