<giggling_dagger>, Giggling Dagger, Giggling Dagger

Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement)

+1 to damage rolls and attack rolls using this magic weapon.
This weapon comes as a pair of two Giggling Daggers.

Giggle giggle. When you make a ranged attack with this dagger, it is completely
silent. Creatures cannot identify the direction from which the dagger was thrown
based on sound. Upon impact, the dagger then projects the sound of a child’s giggle
originating from the point of impact.
