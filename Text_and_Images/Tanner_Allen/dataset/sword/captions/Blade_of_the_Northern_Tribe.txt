<blade_of_the_northern_tribe>, Blade of the Northern Tribe, BLADE OF THE NORTHERN TRIBE

Uncommon Longsword (Mara, Versaie,requresatunemant

Damage 1d8+1 (1d10+1)

Northern Smithing: You receive a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls
made with this weapon

Frost edge: Every day at dawn this sword forms a thin lauer of
razer sharp ice on it's edge. The first five successful attacks you make
deals an extra 1d4 Cold damage. After five hits the layer of ice melts.

A touch of cold: Once pe day as an action you can stick the sword in
a surface of water to create a layer of ice in a 15 foot radius. All creatures
in this area have to make a strength saving throw (DC 12) on a failure
they are locked in the ice and considered restrained. on a success the 
ice lauer breaks compleyely and quickly melts away.
