<rainbow_rapier>, Rainbow Rapier, RARE, REQUIRES ATTUNEMENT

+1 RAPIER. 1d8+1, FINESSE WEAPON.

- (1/DAY) AS AN ACTION, THE WEILDER OF THIS WEAPON
CAN SWING THE BLADE CREATING A WAVE OF COLOURS
AS IF HAVING CAST 'COLOUR SPRAY’ AT ZND LEVEL.

- ON SCORING A CRITICAL HIT, THE WEILDER TURNS
INVISABLE UNTIL THE THE END OF THEIR NEXT TURN.

- THE WEILDER CAN CHANGE THE COLOURS OF ANY OBJECT
OR CREATURE WITH A SINGLE TOUCH OF THIS WEAPON.
THE COLOURS CHANGED CAN BE OF A SPECIFIC DETAIL,
SUCH AS A PERSON'S HAIR OR THE HANDLE OF A DOOR.
