<embrasure>, Embrasure, EMBRASURE

UNCOMMON - BOW

Should I equip a shield? Or a bow? Why not
both!

MECHANICS

As a reaction, you can say the words
"Protect me!", to create a magical shield
around the bow, adding +2 to your AC for
this round. After using this feature two
times, you have to finish a short rest before
using it again.

(Requires proficiency with longbows.)
