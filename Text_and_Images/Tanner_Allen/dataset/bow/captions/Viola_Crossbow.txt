<viola_crossbow>, Viola Crossbow, VIOLA CROSSBOW

Weapon (Light Crossbow), Instrument (Viola), Rare

Cost: 200 gp

Damage: 1d8 piercing

Weight: 5 Ibs

Properties: Ammunition, Loading, (Range 80/320),
‘Two-Handed, Slow

‘The Viola Crossbow serves as the perfect ranged
weapon for a bard, as it doubles as an instrument. Asa
bonus action, the user may switch between Viola and
Crossbow form. The weapon can be loaded with

well as regular crossbow bolts.
