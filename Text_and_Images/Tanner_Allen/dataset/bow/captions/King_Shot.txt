<king_shot>, King Shot, THE KINGS SHOT

RARE - BOW

Items like this, that are infused with the spirits of
animals, sometimes exhibit odd animal like
behaviors.

This bow was infused with the spirit of a massive
black lion, whose roars were so loud they shook the
very ground beneath him.

MECHANICS

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made
with this bow.

As an action, you can speak acommand to produce a
booming noise that emanates from this bow. All
creatures (including yourself) within a 30-foot radius
must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a
failure, the creature is deafened. At the end of each of
its turns, the creature can make a DC 10 Constitution
saving throw. On a success, the condition ends.

This bow has 3 charges, which reset at midnight.
