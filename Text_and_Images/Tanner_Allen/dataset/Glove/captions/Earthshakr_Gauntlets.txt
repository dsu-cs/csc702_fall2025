<earthshakr_gauntlets>, Earthshakr Gauntlets, 

Wondrous Item - Artifact Requires Attunement
Cursed, The gauntlet replaces the users left hand over the course of 3 days. It can
not be removed except by magical means.

The gauntlets grant +1 to AC and Strength

The gauntlet has 5 charges. You can expend a charge to cast one of the following
spells (DC 18/+4) Mold Earth, Earth Tremor, Earthbind, Maximilian's Earthen
Grasp, Erupting Earth, Stone Shape, Wall of Stone, and Transmute Rock.

The gauntlet regains 1D4+3 charges daily, at dawn.
