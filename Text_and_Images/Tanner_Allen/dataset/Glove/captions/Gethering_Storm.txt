<gethering_storm>, HAND WRAPS OF THE GATHERING STORM

Rare Magic Item (Cloth Handwraps) requires attunement by a monk

Damage: (Unarmed Strike) + 1 Bludgeoning

Fast as Lightening: You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls and damage
rolls when making Unarmed Strikes whilst wearing these hand wraps.

Electrifying: These hand wraps can hold 10 charges. You can expend 5
charges whenever you make unarmed strike to deal an extra 2d4+2
Lightning damage. You gain one charge on every (non-electrified)
unarmed strike you land on a hostile creature. You gain two charges
when you land a critical hit. The charges deplete to 0 when you have not
hit a hostile creature in 10 minutes.
