<snowball_gloves>, SNOWBALL GLOVES

Wondrous item, uncommon

A person wielding this gloves can use an action to create a
snowball. This snowballs count as thrown weapons with a range

of 30 ft / 60 ft, dealing 1D4 cold damage + your Dexterity or
Strength modifier. Additionally, if you make a critical hit, the target is
blinded by the snow until the start of your next turn.

Snowballs created by this gloves deals no damage if they’re not
thrown, and they vanish mere seconds after being made.
