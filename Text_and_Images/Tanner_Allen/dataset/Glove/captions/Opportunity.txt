<opportunity>, GLOVES OF OPPORTUNITY

VERY RARE - GAUNTLETS

Always ready to take the opportunity to
steal carried items.

MECHANICS

While wearing these gloves, whenever a
creature fails an attack roll against you
with a critical 1, you can use your reaction
to steal one of the items they are holding
in their hands or wearing around their
neck (your choice).

(Requires Dexterity of 13 or more.)
