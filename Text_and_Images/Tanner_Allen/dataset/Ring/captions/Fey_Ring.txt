<fey_ring>, Fey Ring, RING OF THE FEY

Rare Wondrous Item

Brain of the Fey: When attuned to this ring you gain a +1 bonus to your
intelligence. Additionally you gain a +2 bonus on any Intelligence
(Arcana) and Intelligence (Nature) checks you make.

Fey Ancestry: Whilst attuned to this ring you have advantage on saving
throws against being charmed, and magic can’t put you to sleep.

Light of the Feywild: This ring holds one charge of a special kind of
Faerie Fire. This spell requires no concentration, lasts for 1 minute and is
tints of orange and red. When you are in the Feywild both areas
described in the description of the spell increase by 10ft. You can cast
this spell once per day.
