Banps OF WENWU
Wonderous Item, legendary (requires attunement by a monk)

A set of magical bracers that take the form of ten ancient
metal bands worn around the forearm(s).

While wearing these bracers, you gain a +1 bonus to AC if
you are wearing no armor and using no shield. Additionally
when you make an unarmed strike, your reach is 5 feet
greater than normal and deal an additional 144 force
damage.

‘The braceres have 10 charges that are used to fuel the
spells within them. Wisdom is your spelicasting modifier and
it regains 2d4+2 charges daily at dawn. You can expend 1
charge to cast lightning lure, catapult (bands only),
thunderous smite (unarmed strike only) or shield. When you
cast catapult, you can expend additional charges, up to half
your proficiency bonus (rounded up) to cast at higher levels.
‘The bands return to you whether they hit or not.
