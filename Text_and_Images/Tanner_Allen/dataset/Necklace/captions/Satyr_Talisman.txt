<satyr_talisman>, TALISMAN OF THE SATYR

Uncommon Magic Item, requires attunement

Master of the Bow: When you attack a creature that is within 5ft range
of you with a shortbow, longbow or any type of crossbow, you do not
suffer from disadvantage on the ranged attack roll. Additionally you gain
10 ft on your range (minimum and maximum) with these weapons.

Sounds of the Forest: When attuned to this item you gain proficiency
with Panflutes. With a successful Charisma (Performance) check (DC15)
when playing a panflute, you can attract 1d8 birds from the surroundings.
Most types of birds will happily tweet along to your song.
