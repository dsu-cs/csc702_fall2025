<earing_of_whisper>, EARRING OF WHISPER

Uncommon earring

Jenga: This enchanted earring can send and receive the Message spell
to and from other connectead earrings within 500 ft. To send a message
the wearer must activate the earring by touching it. The earring will
always passively receive messages. If the receiver is out of range they
will hear nothing

Can you hear me now?: Earrings can be connected by physically
holding them together for 10 minutes, this connection is broken if both
earrings are not within a 5 mile radius of each other for at least a week.
