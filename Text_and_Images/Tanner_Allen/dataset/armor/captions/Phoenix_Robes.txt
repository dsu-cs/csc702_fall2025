<phoenix_robes>, Phoenix Robes, LUMINOUS VESTMENTS OF THE PHOENIX

Legendary Robes, requires attunement by a Wizard, Sorcerer, Druid, Mystic or Warlock

Soul of the Fire Plane: When attuned to this robe you are immune to
Fire damage and resistant to Cold damage. You also gain a +4 bonus on
your spellcasting ability.

Flight of the Phoenix: As an action you can cast the spell Fly (6th level)
with these robes. You can use this feature twice per day.

Ashes to Ashes: As an action you can cast the spell Firestorm using
this robe. You can use this feature once every 2 days.
