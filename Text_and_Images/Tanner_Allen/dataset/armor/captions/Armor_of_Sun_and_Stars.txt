<armor_of_sun_and_stars>, Armor of the Sun and Stars

Ac 18
Disadvatage: Stealth

Light of the Sun: YOU CAN CAST SUNBEAN (8TH LEVEL) FROM THIS ARMOR.
YOU CAN USE THIS DEATER AGAIN AFTER 7 DAYS HAVE PASSED

BLESSING OF THE STARS: YOU CAN SPEND A BONUS ACTION TO BLESS UP TO 8 CREATURES WITHIN A 50 FT RANGE. THEY CAN ADD 1D6 FOR THEIR NEXT ATTACK ROLL. THE BLESSING LASTS FOR ONE MINUTE OR UNTIL A CREATURE USES THE BLESSING FOR ITS ATTACK. YOU CAN SPEN ANY NUMBER OF CHARGES IN ONE BNUS ACTION BUT BLESSING DO NOT STACK. AT MIDNIGH THE ARMOR RAGINS 1D6 CHARGES.

HEAVENLY LIGHT: YOUR MELEE WEAPON ATTACKS DEAL AN EXTRA 2 RADIANT DAMAGE. AS LONG AS YOU HAVE AT LEAST ONE CHARGE OF EITHER LIGHT OF THE SUN OR BLESSING OF THE STARS.
